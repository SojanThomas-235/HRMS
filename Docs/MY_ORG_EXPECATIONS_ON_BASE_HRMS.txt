# HRMS Module Enhancement Requirements

## Time Reporting, Employee Data Ownership, and Rewards Model

### Overview

The standard Salesforce HRMS module provides traditional employee management, attendance, and time reporting capabilities. Our organization requires an enhanced HRMS model that moves beyond conventional timesheet-based workforce management.

The proposed enhancements focus on three major areas:

1. Intelligent Time Reporting Model
2. Employee Data Ownership Model
3. Long-Term Employee Rewards Model

These enhancements are intended to recognize employee capability, knowledge, accountability, and long-term value creation instead of measuring contribution solely through hours worked.

---

# 1. Intelligent Time Reporting Model

## Business Objective

Traditional HRMS systems assume that employee productivity is proportional to the number of hours worked (e.g., 8 or 8.5 hours per day). This approach does not accurately measure the value delivered by experienced professionals.

Our HRMS should instead reward knowledge, efficiency, and successful delivery rather than time spent.

---

## 1.1 Employee Base Value Calculation

Each employee shall have a **Base Professional Value (BPV)** that represents their market value independent of daily time reporting.

The BPV shall be calculated using multiple weighted factors, including:

### Academic Qualifications

* Highest educational qualification
* 12th Grade
* Diploma
* Undergraduate Degree (3-Year)
* Undergraduate Degree (4-Year)
* Master's Degree
* Double Master's Degree
* MBA
* M.Tech / MS
* PhD
* Post-Doctoral Fellowship
* Professional Certifications

Each qualification contributes a predefined percentage toward the employee's Base Professional Value.

---

### Professional Experience

Additional weighting shall be provided based on:

* Total years of experience
* Domain experience
* Enterprise experience
* Global organization experience
* Leadership experience

---

### Organization Profile

Experience gained from different organization types shall also contribute to employee value.

Examples include:

* Startup
* Mid-size organization
* Enterprise organization
* Fortune 500 companies
* Government organizations
* Research organizations

---

### Technical Skills

The employee profile shall maintain:

* Technologies mastered
* Programming languages
* Platforms
* Frameworks
* Cloud certifications
* Tools
* Methodologies
* Industry certifications

Skill proficiency shall directly influence the employee's Base Professional Value.

---

### Base Professional Value Formula

The HRMS shall derive a Base Professional Value using configurable weighted parameters.

Example:

Base Professional Value =
Education Score +
Experience Score +
Organization Score +
Technical Skill Score +
Certification Score

The weighting model shall be configurable by HR administrators.

---

# 2. Modern Time Reporting Model

## Business Objective

Time reporting should serve only as a minimal operational record rather than the primary measure of employee contribution.

The organization values outcomes more than hours logged.

---

## Key Principles

Employees are not expected to artificially report 8–8.5 hours daily if assigned work has already been completed to the required quality.

Instead, the HRMS shall record:

* Assigned work
* Delivered work
* Quality of output
* Completion status
* Time consumed (for reference only)

---

### Outcome-Based Reporting

Employees may report:

* 1 hour
* 2 hours
* 4 hours
* 6 hours

provided the assigned deliverables have been completed according to expected quality standards.

Completion of work—not occupancy of time—should determine performance.

---

### Salary Structure

Salary shall contain two components:

#### Component 1 – Base Professional Value

This constitutes the majority of employee compensation.

It is determined by:

* qualifications
* experience
* skills
* expertise
* market value

This component remains largely independent of daily time reporting.

---

#### Component 2 – Time Reporting Value

This is a relatively small variable component.

It compensates employees for operational work tracking but is not intended to dominate overall salary.

---

### Efficiency-Based Evaluation

The HRMS shall recognize employees who consistently complete work efficiently.

Example:

Employee A

* 15 years experience
* Completes task in 2 hours
* High quality
* Uses advanced expertise

Employee B

* Same experience
* Completes same task in 6 hours

Under the proposed model:

Employee A should receive:

* Lower time-reporting compensation
* Higher annual performance incentives
* Higher efficiency score

Employee B should receive:

* Higher time-reporting allocation
* Lower efficiency incentives if the additional time does not result in higher business value

The objective is to reward efficiency rather than prolonged task duration.

---

# 3. Employee Data Ownership Model

## Business Objective

Modern organizations generate value through data, software, documentation, automation, and digital assets.

Employees who create these assets should remain accountable for their quality while also being recognized for their long-term contribution.

---

## Ownership Responsibilities

Every employee-created asset shall include ownership metadata.

Examples:

* Source code
* Documents
* Designs
* Automation scripts
* AI prompts
* Configuration
* Architecture
* Knowledge articles
* Data models
* Reports
* Dashboards

Each asset shall record:

* Creator
* Contributors
* Reviewer
* Approver
* Owner
* Creation date
* Version history

---

### Accountability

Employees remain accountable for:

* correctness
* documentation
* maintainability
* traceability

even after project completion.

If an employee leaves the organization, ownership transitions operationally to the organization, but historical authorship and accountability remain permanently recorded.

---

### Documentation Requirement

Every owned asset must include:

* documentation
* implementation guide
* maintenance instructions
* dependencies
* usage instructions
* version history

This ensures future maintainability and business continuity.

---

# 4. Employee Reward and Royalty Model

## Business Objective

Employees should benefit from the long-term value generated by assets they create.

Rather than recognizing contributions only once through salary, the organization may provide ongoing rewards for assets that continue to create measurable business value.

---

## Reward Framework

Where organizational policy permits, employees may receive long-term incentives tied to the measurable success of their contributed assets.

Examples include:

* revenue generated
* operational cost savings
* productivity improvements
* customer adoption
* intellectual property value
* strategic business impact

The reward model may be configured as:

* annual bonus
* profit-sharing
* royalty
* innovation incentive
* long-term recognition award

The exact calculation method shall be configurable by HR and Finance.

---

## Beneficiary Preference

Employees shall be able to nominate beneficiaries for any eligible long-term rewards.

Beneficiaries may include:

* spouse
* children
* family members
* legal heirs
* charitable organizations
* NGOs
* educational institutions
* orphanages
* trusts

If no beneficiary has been designated, any remaining benefits shall be handled according to applicable organizational policy and legal requirements.

---

# 5. HRMS Functional Enhancements

The enhanced HRMS shall include:

* Base Professional Value engine
* Configurable qualification scoring
* Experience scoring engine
* Technical skill scoring
* Organization experience scoring
* Outcome-based task reporting
* Minimal time reporting
* Productivity analytics
* Efficiency scoring
* Data ownership registry
* Asset traceability
* Contribution tracking
* Documentation compliance
* Long-term reward management
* Beneficiary nomination management
* Royalty and incentive calculation engine
* HR and Finance administration dashboard

---

# Expected Business Benefits

* Rewards knowledge instead of hours worked.
* Encourages efficient task completion.
* Recognizes highly skilled professionals appropriately.
* Improves accountability for digital assets and organizational knowledge.
* Preserves ownership history and traceability of all work products.
* Supports long-term employee engagement through configurable recognition and incentive models.
* Aligns compensation with value creation rather than time spent alone.
