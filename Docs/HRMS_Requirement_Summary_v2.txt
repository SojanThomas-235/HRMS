# HRMS Requirement Summary (Revised v2)
# Custom ERP Application — Built from Scratch

---

## Objective

The organization wants to build a modern, custom HRMS application that evaluates employees based on their knowledge, skills, experience, quality of work, accountability, and long-term contribution — rather than only on the number of hours they work.

---

## Core Requirements

---

### 1. Base Professional Value (BPV) Engine

Develop a Base Professional Value (BPV) Engine that calculates an employee's professional worth using configurable scoring criteria.

Scoring factors include:

**Academic Qualifications**
- 12th Grade
- Diploma
- Undergraduate Degree (3-Year / 4-Year)
- Master's Degree / Double Master's
- MBA
- M.Tech / MS
- PhD
- Post-Doctoral Fellowship
- Professional Certifications

**Professional Experience**
- Total years of experience
- Domain experience
- Enterprise experience
- Global organization experience
- Leadership experience

**Previous Organization Profile**
- Startup
- Mid-size organization
- Enterprise organization
- Fortune 500 companies
- Government organizations
- Research organizations

**Technical Skills**
- Programming languages
- Platforms and frameworks
- Cloud certifications
- Tools and methodologies
- Industry certifications

The BPV shall be computed as a weighted sum of all scoring factors. Weights must be configurable by HR administrators.

The BPV forms the major component of an employee's overall compensation.

---

### 2. Outcome-Based Time Reporting

Replace the traditional "8-hour workday" model with an outcome-based reporting system.

Employees report:
- Assigned tasks
- Completed tasks
- Quality of work
- Completion status
- Time spent (for reference only, not the primary measure)

Employees may report 1, 2, 4, or 6 hours for a day, provided assigned deliverables have been completed to the required quality standard.

Performance is measured based on successful delivery and quality — not the number of hours worked.

---

### 3. Dual-Component Salary Structure

Employee compensation is structured into two distinct components:

**Component 1 — Base Professional Value Salary (Majority)**
- Constitutes the majority of total compensation
- Derived from the employee's BPV score
- Reflects qualifications, experience, skills, and market value
- Stable and largely independent of daily time reporting

**Component 2 — Time Reporting Value (Minority)**
- A small variable component
- Compensates for operational time tracking
- Does not dominate overall salary

This structure ensures that experienced, high-quality employees are compensated primarily for their professional capability — not for occupying hours.

---

### 4. Efficiency Evaluation

Introduce an Efficiency Score that recognizes employees who complete work faster while maintaining high quality.

Example:
- Employee A (15 years experience) completes a task in 2 hours at high quality → receives higher efficiency score and annual performance incentives.
- Employee B (same experience) takes 6 hours for the same task → receives higher time-reporting allocation but lower efficiency incentives if the extra time does not produce additional business value.

The system rewards expertise and productivity. It does not incentivize prolonged task duration.

---

### 5. Employee Data Ownership

Every digital asset created by an employee must maintain complete ownership metadata throughout its lifecycle.

Assets include:
- Source code
- Documents
- Designs
- Reports
- Dashboards
- Data models
- Automation scripts
- Knowledge articles
- Architecture documents
- AI prompts
- Configuration files

Each asset must record:
- Creator
- Contributors
- Reviewer
- Approver
- Owner
- Creation date
- Version history

**Post-Departure Rule:**
If an employee leaves the organization, operational ownership transfers to the organization. However, historical authorship and individual accountability remain permanently recorded in the system. This ensures full traceability and compliance throughout the asset's lifecycle.

---

### 6. Documentation Compliance

Every owned asset must include complete documentation as a mandatory requirement.

Required documentation:
- Implementation guide
- User guide
- Maintenance instructions
- Dependencies
- Version history

This supports knowledge sharing, future maintainability, and business continuity. Incomplete documentation must be flagged by the system.

---

### 7. Long-Term Rewards

Provide a configurable reward mechanism for employees whose work continues to generate measurable business value over time.

Reward basis may include:
- Revenue generated by the asset
- Operational cost savings
- Productivity improvements
- Customer adoption
- Intellectual property value
- Strategic business impact

Reward types may include:
- Annual bonuses
- Innovation incentives
- Profit sharing
- Royalty-based rewards
- Long-term recognition programs

The calculation method and eligibility criteria shall be fully configurable by HR and Finance administrators.

---

### 8. Beneficiary Management

Allow employees to nominate beneficiaries who may receive eligible long-term rewards according to organizational policy and applicable legal requirements.

Beneficiaries may include:
- Spouse
- Children
- Other family members / legal heirs
- Charitable organizations
- NGOs
- Educational institutions
- Orphanages
- Trusts

If no beneficiary is designated, remaining benefits are handled per organizational policy and legal requirements.

---

## Functional Modules Required

The HRMS application shall include the following modules:

- Employee Master
- Base Professional Value (BPV) Engine
- Qualification & Experience Scoring
- Technical Skill & Certification Management
- Outcome-Based Task Reporting
- Time Reporting
- Productivity & Efficiency Analytics
- Dual-Component Salary Management
- Data Ownership Registry
- Asset & Documentation Management
- Contribution Tracking
- Reward & Incentive Management (Royalty Calculation Engine)
- Beneficiary Management
- HR & Finance Administration Dashboard
- Reports & Analytics

---

## Expected Business Benefits

- Rewards expertise and knowledge rather than time spent.
- Encourages faster, high-quality task completion.
- Aligns compensation with professional value and market worth.
- Improves accountability for all organizational digital assets.
- Preserves ownership, authorship, and contribution history permanently.
- Ensures documentation and knowledge are never lost when employees leave.
- Strengthens long-term employee engagement through configurable recognition and incentive models.
- Aligns employee rewards directly with the value they create for the organization.

---

## One-Line Summary

Build a value-driven custom HRMS that measures employees by their professional capability, work quality, efficiency, asset ownership, and long-term business contribution — supported by a dual-component salary structure and a permanent ownership and accountability registry.
